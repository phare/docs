Phare Uptime monitors your websites, APIs, servers and cron jobs, and alerts you when something goes wrong, so you can fix it before your users notice.

## Uptime monitoring

Monitors check your websites, APIs and servers from multiple regions at a regular interval. Phare sends HTTP requests, opens TCP connections, or [pings your servers](/uptime/monitors#icmp) with ICMP, and creates an incident when a check fails.

[Learn more about uptime monitoring](/uptime/monitors).

## Cron job monitoring

Cron job monitoring (also known as heartbeat monitoring) is best for jobs that can't be checked from the outside, like a nightly backup, a scheduled task or a worker. Your service sends a ping to Phare each time it runs, and Phare creates an incident when the ping is late, missing, or reports a failure.

[Learn more about heartbeats](/uptime/heartbeats).

## Incident management

Phare creates an incident when a monitor or a heartbeat fails, and resolves it when everything is back to normal. Each incident keeps the timeline of what happened, and lets you post updates and write a post-mortem.

[Learn more about incident management](/uptime/incidents).
